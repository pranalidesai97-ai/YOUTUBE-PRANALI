If you're in your twenties right now, this one is for you.

Our twenties are full of figuring things out: relationships, finances, mental health, physical health. We often look to other people for help and advice, and in the process we forget to look into ourselves. I'll be turning thirty next year, so in this video I'm sharing the five lessons I learned in my twenties that I wish someone had told me sooner.

In this video:
1. Don't wait to be rescued
2. Take control of your finances
3. Find your happiness and a creative outlet
4. Know yourself
5. Learn to make your own decisions

Until next time, be confident, be bold, and keep choosing yourself.
